## Public scope

The release contains 12 executable paths: spatial quality control, conditional networks, ISPAT, GP-GHS, ISPAT-3D, spatial machine learning, spatial clustering, neural prediction, scalar-on-image screening, image-to-image screening, mediation, and tumor-shape PCA.

The software reports numerical/statistical outputs and reproducibility artifacts. It does not explain results, assign scientific or biological meaning, or make clinical conclusions. Study-specific interpretation is reserved for direct discussion or collaboration with Sagnik Bhadury.

Only already-public methods and repositories belong in the public application. Do not add working manuscripts, private-repository ideas, confidential research directions, protected data, or undisclosed LLM/SAE work.
