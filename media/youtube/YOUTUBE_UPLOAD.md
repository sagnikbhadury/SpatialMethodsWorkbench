## Description

Spatial Methods Workbench v0.2.1 is an open-source, citation-aware Shiny environment for statistically motivated and biologically grounded spatial and imaging analysis. This detailed tutorial covers data preparation, method selection, all 12 guided workflows, synthetic-data execution, output auditing, local installation, advanced public engines, and reproducibility bundles.

The Workbench supports:

- global spatial autocorrelation with Moran's I;
- ridge-stabilized conditional-network screening;
- ISPat shared and zone-specific 2D spatial networks;
- GP-GHS continuously spatially varying graphical models;
- ISPat 3D networks for registered volumes;
- linear and logistic prediction;
- spatially weighted clustering;
- shallow neural prediction;
- scalar-image ridge screening;
- latent image-to-image screening;
- model-based mediation analysis; and
- contour and shape PCA.

Applicable analysis-ready inputs include cell, spot, subcellular, pixel, voxel, subject, contour, section, and volume-level records derived from multiplex imaging, spatial transcriptomics or proteomics, registered serial sections, aligned biomedical images, and tabular exposures or outcomes. The application does not process raw instrument data and must not receive protected health information on the public service.
